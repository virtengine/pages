07 — Motion, budgets, guardrails

Motion only where it explains something.

Signature moments

det.io — section rules draw outward from the register mark like a plotter (scaleX, 320ms).

identity — pointer-tracked foil on the Proof Card (≤60 lines of vanilla JS); and one travelling token in the “what leaves the phone” flow while every other field stays put and desaturates.

Motion rules

120–320ms · cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1) · transform/opacity only · no bounce/elastic · no pulsing dots · no autoplay marquee · everything inside prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference.

Budgets

≤4 font files & ≤160KB woff2/page · ≤5KB JS · ≤40KB CSS gz · LCP ≤2.0s · CLS ≤0.05 · WCAG 2.2 AA · Markdown-for-Agents contract preserved.

Remove (det.io)
  • ✕Auto-scrolling ticker bands → static justified credit line
  • ✕Cream #F5F0E2 → neutral stock #f1efe9
  • ✕Uppercase H1 + crushed tracking
  • ✕Offset 5px 5px 0 shadows on every card → plates only
  • ✕Lattice plus-grid “engineering paper” → ruled measurement behind figures
  • ✕Ambient grain / halftone over text
  • ✕Space Grotesk + Inter + JetBrains Mono
Add (identity.org.au)
  • ✓Editorial register (Newsreader) for heroes + 25 reading pages
  • ✓Guilloché grounds seeded from record numbers
