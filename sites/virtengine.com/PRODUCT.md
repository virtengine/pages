# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Buyers of compute and services who order infrastructure directly at published
prices, by bid, or matched to a specification. Providers who list compute
resources and manage their offerings. Protocol participants who read the
technical documentation and follow network status. Technical evaluators
assessing whether VirtEngine is credible and open.

## Product Purpose

VirtEngine is an open-source infrastructure-marketplace protocol stewarded by
DETIO FOUNDATION LTD, an Australian public-benefit not-for-profit. The site
exists to explain the protocol, show how ordering and settlement work, and give
evaluators verifiable evidence — it is the front door to a network that is still
being built, not a storefront for a live service.

Success means an evaluator can understand what the protocol does, how identity
and escrow fit, and when the network goes live, without taking the project's
word for anything.

## Positioning

The protocol is open-source and stewarded by a public-benefit not-for-profit
rather than a commercial operator, and infrastructure is ordered at published
prices, by bid, or by specification match. Identity, escrow, and settlement are
on-chain. A neighbouring marketplace could not truthfully copy the combination
of open-source stewardship plus direct/bid/matched ordering.

## Operating Context

- Multi-site Astro monorepo: `virtengine.com`, `docs.virtengine.com`, `det.io`,
  `identity.org.au` (`sites/*`).
- Astro + Tailwind v4, pnpm, Node 24. Local preview: `astro preview` on
  127.0.0.1:4181.
- `DESIGN.md` at the site root is normative for tokens, logo, artwork, and
  accessibility. It is the incumbent design authority — do not supersede it.
- Figures are built as components in `src/components/art/`,
  `src/components/diagrams/`, `src/components/brand/`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **The network is not live.** Copy must state "In development — the network is
  not live". TestNet is January 2027; MainNet is March 2027. These are fixed
  facts; grep for `March 202` before changing any date.
- Zero-JS interaction patterns are a deliberate constraint (`:target` tab
  states), not an oversight.
- The V icon is a fixed brand asset and is never redrawn or approximated.
- Every triangle motif points down, like a "V". An apex-up triangle is a defect.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: VirtEngine, "by DET.io". Voice is technical and precise; grey
  typography, green `#60CC5D` as the single accent, engineering-paper surfaces.
- The brand mark is the original logo asset; the site uses the real assets rather
  than inventing lookalikes.
- Photography (CC0/PDM, treated as duotone) is the site's "real world" layer.
  AI-generated imagery is not used.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real implementation: the site builds 148 pages; `scripts/check-local-links.py`
  and `scripts/check-structured-data.mjs` pass.
- Media provenance in `public/media/manifest.json`, regenerated by
  `scripts/build-media-data.py`.
- `src/data/status.ts` is the source of truth for network status.
- Absences that must not be fabricated: there are no customer testimonials, no
  usage metrics, and no live network data — the network has not launched.

## Product Principles

1. Verifiable over promotional. Show mechanism and evidence; do not claim
   traction that does not exist.
2. Open by construction. Source, licence, and governance are part of the pitch.
3. One brand system across all four sites, each with its own voice and audience.
4. The design language is engineering paper and precision instruments — not
   generic SaaS.
5. Accessibility is a requirement, not a polish pass.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA with a documented contrast table in `DESIGN.md` §9. Figures use
sparse, high-contrast mono labels. Readable type sizes are a hard requirement —
no tiny, low-contrast labels anywhere in the interface.
