# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Grantmakers and government research funders evaluating whether DET.io is a
credible Australian research non-profit. Researchers and policy readers looking
for its published work on decentralised cloud, AI safety, and digital identity.
Partners and auditors who need the foundation's registration, governance, and
financial-reporting facts.

## Product Purpose

DETIO FOUNDATION LTD (ACN 699 651 771) is an Australian public-benefit
not-for-profit researching decentralised cloud, AI safety, and digital identity.
det.io is the foundation's public face: it publishes the research, states the
governance, and makes the organisation auditable.

Success means a funder or auditor can verify the foundation's identity,
governance, and published research from the site alone.

## Positioning

A registered Australian not-for-profit publishing primary research on
decentralised cloud, AI safety, and digital identity, with governance and
financial reporting published in the open. A commercial vendor would not publish
its governance and financials alongside advocacy.

## Operating Context

- Astro site inside the `pages` monorepo; shares the VirtEngine brand system but
  has its own editorial voice and palette.
- `DESIGN.md` is normative for surface and type (Amendment B, 2026-09-25).
- Editorial type: Instrument Serif display over warm paper surfaces
  (`#f1efe9`, `#e7e4db`, `#fbfaf7`), ink `#14171c`.
- Registration documents and examination reports are part of the evidence base.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The foundation's registration details, ACN, and governance statements are fixed
  facts and must not be paraphrased loosely.
- Claims about research must stay scoped to what the published work supports.
- Sister sites in the monorepo (`virtengine.com`, `docs.virtengine.com`,
  `identity.org.au`) share brand assets but not editorial voice.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: DET.io, operated by DETIO FOUNDATION LTD. Voice is institutional,
  evidence-led, and plain — a research foundation, not a startup.
- Warm paper surfaces and serif display type, deliberately distinct from
  VirtEngine's instrument aesthetic.

## Evidence on Hand

- Published research pages and examination reports under `src/pages/research/`.
- Foundation registration and governance documents.
- Absences that must not be fabricated: no customer metrics and no claim of
  commercial deployment.

## Product Principles

1. Publish the evidence, including the inconvenient parts.
2. Registration and governance are content, not fine print.
3. Institutional register without institutional coldness.
4. Keep this site visually distinct from its sister sites while staying one
   brand system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Long-form research reading requires comfortable measure, clear
heading hierarchy, and readable body type.
