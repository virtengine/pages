Tenants are the demand side of VirtEngine: teams and individuals who need
compute — containers, virtual machines, GPU capacity, or HPC clusters — and
lease it from competing providers on the open marketplace.

## Why deploy on a decentralized marketplace

- **Competitive pricing** — providers bid against each other for your
  order; you set the acceptable price bounds.
- **Verifiable providers** — audits, benchmarks, and reviews are on-chain
  evidence, not marketing claims
  (see [Choosing Providers](/tenants/choosing-providers/)).
- **Pay for verified usage** — funds sit in escrow you control and move
  only after signed usage records survive a 24-hour dispute window.
- **A real dispute process** — if a provider's usage claims look wrong, you
  can dispute them on-chain before payment, with immutable evidence.
- **HPC without procurement** — submit SLURM-class jobs to registered
  clusters with a budget cap instead of negotiating cluster access
  (see [Submitting HPC Jobs](/tenants/hpc-jobs/)).

## Your lifecycle in five objects

| Object | What it is | Your control point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deployment | Your workload description on-chain | Create, update, close |
| Order | The open request providers bid on | Placement & pricing constraints |
| Lease | Binding agreement with the winning provider | Accept bid; close anytime |
| Escrow account | Funds backing your leases | Deposit, top up; unspent funds are yours |
| Usage records | The provider's billed claims | 24-hour dispute window on every record |

## Costs

You pay the agreed lease price for verified usage. Marketplace settlement has
0% protocol commission; low validator transaction fees apply only to the
on-chain actions. Your total exposure
is capped by what you put in escrow: an empty escrow account closes the
lease rather than accruing debt.
