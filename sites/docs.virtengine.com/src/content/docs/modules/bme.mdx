`x/bme` is the **Burn/Mint Engine**. It uses VirtEngine's own native asset
and service credit; it does not require the Akash protocol or its compute token.

| Unit | Name | Minimal denomination | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VE | VirtEngine native asset | `uve` | Native monetary unit; fees and bonded stake. |
| VCC | VirtEngine Compute Credit | `uvcc` | Service-accounting unit for the proposed conversion path. |

Client display configurations use six decimal places. A denomination does not
establish a peg, operational redemption, collateral adequacy, or a conversion rate.

The oracle field is now `native_price_feed_id`, with no hard-coded default VE
feed identifier. An unconfigured Pyth contract config fails validation. A valid
native feed must be explicitly configured before a conversion can rely on it.

## Implementation status

Reviewed against repository source on **3 October 2026**:

- `BurnMint`, `MintVCC`, and `BurnVCC` in
  [`msg_server.go`](https://github.com/virtengine/virtengine/blob/main/x/bme/keeper/msg_server.go)
  return **pending** responses. They do not perform the proposed oracle-priced
  bank conversion, persist a completed conversion, or enforce its complete
  collateral/circuit-breaker flow. Pending is not evidence of executed conversion.
- `MintTokens` and `BurnTokens` in
  [`fee_collection.go`](https://github.com/virtengine/virtengine/blob/main/x/bme/keeper/fee_collection.go)
  provide bank operations and cumulative accounting. Their existence does not
  prove integration into every conversion or service-payment path.
- `SettleBilling` in
  [`settlement.go`](https://github.com/virtengine/virtengine/blob/main/x/bme/keeper/settlement.go)
  transfers available escrow funds to providers, minus a configurable fee that
  it burns. `DefaultSettleSpreadBps` is **0**. The default path burns none of the
  payment. Any adopted configuration must reconcile this configurable path with
  the provider-facing policy of full agreed payout and separate transaction fees.

## Primary issuance and conversion differ

The [proposed issuance policy](/concepts/tokenomics/) starts with zero supply
and no fixed cap. Eligible human participation originates proposed primary
allocations: 14 units of each 15-unit batch to eligible humans and 1 to
Foundation stewardship. Staking does not independently authorize new primary issuance.

A service-credit conversion is **not another personhood allocation**. Refunds,
redemption and native reissuance need separate specifications and accounting.
The current conversion stubs do not implement the full human issuance policy.
Transferable payments can also be received by providers and other economic actors.

## Messages and CLI

| Message | Intended operation | Current handler result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `MsgBurnMint` | Paired denomination conversion | Pending; conversion unimplemented. |
| `MsgMintVCC` | Convert native VE into VCC | Pending; conversion unimplemented. |
| `MsgBurnVCC` | Redeem unused VCC toward native VE | Pending; conversion unimplemented. |
| `MsgUpdateParams` | Authority-controlled parameter update | Validates and stores parameters. |

```sh
virtengine tx bme mint-vcc 1000000uve --from mykey
virtengine tx bme burn-vcc 1000000uvcc --from mykey
virtengine tx bme burn-mint 1000000uve uvcc --from mykey
```

These illustrate the renamed command/API surface, not an operational conversion
service. Queries include `Params`, `Status`, and `VaultState`.

## Net consumption is a hypothesis to test

Track **native units burned minus native units reissued** over a defined
interval, alongside outstanding credits, refunds, reserves, and provider payments.
An equal reissue offsets a gross burn. A provider transfer changes ownership,
not supply. Destroying a service credit does not itself prove net VE destruction.

A permanent consumption link could create recurring demand for human-originated
claims. The current conversion handlers do not establish it. Outside compute,
reserves, common ownership, entitlement capture, scarcity, and governance changes
can weaken that link. Try the [interactive economic experiments](https://det.io/learn/economic-invariants)
and [research synthesis](https://det.io/research/human-machine-symbiosis).

## Compatibility and adoption

This rename changes messages, RPC methods, CLI commands, wallet metadata, and
the service-credit denomination. Field numbers are retained, but renamed type
URLs and RPC names are an **API-breaking change**. Rebuild old signed conversion
transactions with the new SDK. Source naming does not migrate bank balances.

Fresh development genesis should use `uve` and `uvcc`. Existing networks require
a coordinated upgrade and audited migration of bank supply/metadata/balances,
escrow, BME records, configured limits and clients. No live migration is performed
by this rename. Python and Rust outputs remain experimental under
`sdk/generation/toolchain.json`.

## Remaining work

Specify prices and units, rounding, collateral, oracle freshness, credit
transferability, redemption, authorization and atomic failure. Implement and
test the handlers, then align simulations with primary-issuance policy and test
refunds, price shocks, reserves and provider/agent common ownership. “Equilibrium”
describes a design aspiration, not an observed outcome.
