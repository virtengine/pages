title: Governance & Staking
description: How consensus governs VirtEngine parameters and how conservative staking incentives support network security.
---

Governance is how VirtEngine changes protocol parameters. Validators and other
eligible participants adopt changes through network consensus rather than a
Foundation administrator changing a central service.

## Staking

Staking remains a security mechanism for validators and delegators. The
proposed model reduces staking issuance by roughly 90% from the prior design;
reward rates, commissions, unbonding and slashing terms are governed chain
parameters, not fixed return promises.

## Identity-led issuance

The proposed primary issuance path is an allocation to accounts that meet the
network-defined threshold for a unique verified identity and stay active over
time. The illustrative policy uses quarterly activity checks over a 50-year
horizon. Each 15-token VEID issuance batch allocates 14 tokens to eligible
humans and 1 token to DETIO FOUNDATION LTD's Foundation-controlled genesis
account. The Foundation allocation is part of that batch, not additional
issuance. Consensus may amend all of these parameters.

## VEID and the Foundation

The Foundation operates its websites and reference services, including
identity.org.au. It does not operate VEID as a central identity provider. VEID
is decentralised protocol technology that participating validators may join
and operate under the network rules.

## Participating safely

Before staking or relying on an allocation rule, check the current chain
parameters and adopted governance proposals. No documentation page is a
guarantee of eligibility, price or yield.
