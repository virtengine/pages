title: Benchmarks & Audits

On VirtEngine, reputation is not a star rating in a database someone else
controls. It is on-chain evidence — attestations, measurements, and reviews
tied to verified identities — and tenants filter orders by it.

## The three reputation signals

### Audits (`x/audit`)

Independent auditors publish signed attestations about your provider record:
that your infrastructure, practices, or compliance posture meet a defined
bar. Because tenants can **require audited providers** in their orders, an
audit attestation is effectively a license to bid on a segment of the
market. Attestations are living objects — they can be revoked or superseded,
so staying audited is an ongoing obligation.

### Benchmarks (`x/benchmark`)

Benchmark results are recorded on-chain, converting performance claims into
verifiable data. Benchmarks matter twice:

1. **Selection** — tenants weigh benchmark data when evaluating bids.
2. **Verification** — a provider whose delivered performance diverges from
   its benchmarked profile invites disputes and fraud scrutiny.

### Reviews (`x/review`)

Tenants review providers they have actually leased from. Reviews are tied to
verified VEID identities and real lease history, which makes both fake
praise and drive-by sabotage expensive to fake.

## How reputation affects revenue

| Signal | Market effect |
| --- | --- |
| Valid audit attestation | Qualify for audit-gated orders (often the highest-value segment) |
| Strong benchmarks | Win bids at higher prices than unbenchmarked competitors |
| Positive review history | Reduce tenant risk perception; improve match rates |
| Revoked audit / poor reviews | Shrinking qualified order set; price pressure |

## Practical guidance

- **Get audited before you need it.** Audit-gated orders won't wait for your
  audit to complete.
- **Benchmark honestly.** Benchmarks you can't sustain in production create
  reconciliation discrepancies — and discrepancies above 25% block
  settlement outright.
- **Treat reviews as telemetry.** Recurring review complaints usually
  predict future disputes; fix the cause before it reaches the
  [dispute workflow](/concepts/escrow-settlement/#disputes-and-corrections).
- **Keep your provider attributes current.** Stale region, capability, or
  endpoint data causes failed deployments, and failed deployments become
  reviews.
