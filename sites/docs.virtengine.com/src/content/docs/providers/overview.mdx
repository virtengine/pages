Providers are the supply side of VirtEngine: operators with real capacity —
a datacenter, a Kubernetes cluster, spare GPU nodes, or an HPC facility —
who lease it on the open marketplace and are paid through on-chain escrow.

## What you're signing up for

As a provider you will:

- register an on-chain identity with attributes describing your capacity,
- run the **provider daemon** alongside your infrastructure,
- bid on tenant orders (or publish HPC offerings and queues),
- deploy and operate tenant workloads,
- submit **signed usage reports** on a schedule, and
- receive the full agreed escrow payout in ACT, with no settlement take-rate.

Your reputation — audits, benchmarks, reviews — accumulates on-chain and
directly affects which orders you can win.

## Prerequisites

| Requirement | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Verified identity | VEID enrollment for the operator account — see [VEID Overview](/veid/overview/) |
| Infrastructure | Kubernetes for containerized workloads and/or an HPC scheduler (SLURM, MOAB, Open OnDemand) |
| Chain access | A synced `virtengine` node (own or trusted RPC) |
| Keys | Provider signing key for transactions and usage-report signatures |
| Connectivity | A stable public endpoint for tenant/lease communication, secured via `x/cert` mTLS |

## Onboarding path

<Steps>

1. **Review provider eligibility.** Provider registration may have role or
   identity checks in the current protocol. These checks apply to operating a
   provider account and do not require tenants to pass VEID to browse or use
   every marketplace offer. A provider's buyer proof requirements belong on
   the individual offer and depend on deployed protocol and client support.

2. **Register on-chain.** Create your provider record (`x/provider`) with
   attributes — region, capabilities, contact endpoint. For HPC capacity,
   also register your cluster:

   ```json
   {
     "schema_version": "1.0",
     "name": "A100-east",
     "cluster_type": "slurm",
     "region": "us-east-1",
     "endpoint": "https://hpc.example.com",
     "total_nodes": 64,
     "total_gpus": 512
   }
   ```

   ```bash
   virtengine tx hpc register-provider provider.json --from provider
   ```

3. **Deploy the provider daemon.** Configure it against your chain node and
   your infrastructure — see [Provider Daemon](/providers/provider-daemon/).

4. **Enable usage reporting and settlement.** Without them you will not be
   paid — see [Usage Reporting & Payouts](/providers/usage-and-payments/).

5. **Get audited and benchmarked.** Optional but commercially important:
   auditor attestations and benchmark results widen the set of orders you
   qualify for — see [Benchmarks & Audits](/providers/benchmarks-audits/).

6. **Start bidding.** Tune your pricing to win profitable leases — see
   [Pricing & Bidding Strategy](/providers/pricing-bidding/).

</Steps>

## Revenue model at a glance

```text
payout = settled_usage_value                         # full agreed amount
```

Payment arrives when usage records settle (hourly settlement cycles, 24-hour
dispute window) — not when the lease is signed. Plan cash flow accordingly.

## Operational bar

Tenants and the protocol both hold you to measurable standards: reconciliation
discrepancies above 25% **block settlement**, fraud signals can shut down
leases, and audit/review history is permanent. Read
[Security Obligations](/providers/security/) before going to production.
